🌍 About This Project

Smart Waste AI is designed to support responsible waste segregation by using AI-based assistance to provide useful disposal information.

The project focuses primarily on SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production.

🛡️ Responsible AI

The system should provide clear and responsible guidance. Users should verify disposal requirements with their local waste-management rules when necessary.
